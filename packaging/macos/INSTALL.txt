pdf.SMT for macOS

Choose aarch64 for Apple silicon (M-series), or x86_64 for an Intel Mac.
Open the DMG, then drag pdf.SMT.app into Applications. Eject the DMG and
open pdf.SMT from Applications. Requires macOS 11 or later.

Personal builds without an Apple Developer certificate are ad-hoc signed
and not notarized. macOS may block the first launch. Review the developer
warning under System Settings > Privacy & Security, and use Open Anyway
only if you trust this build. Do not disable Gatekeeper globally.

To make pdf.SMT the default PDF app: select a PDF in Finder, choose Get Info,
select pdf.SMT under Open with, then click Change All. Finder may show
first-page previews instead of document icons when previews are enabled.

Help > About contains the personal welcome message and SMT portrait.
